## Resolved Defect

The historical generated Folium artifact used a 430px open route selector at
left 74px. At a 390px viewport its measured right edge was 504px, so 114px of
the panel was outside the viewport even though fixed positioning did not
increase document `scrollWidth`.

`src/itinerary_system/blueprint_route_selector.py` now adds one mobile-only
rule below 520px:

- left gutter: 12px;
- expanded width: `calc(100vw - 24px)`;
- desktop rule retained: left 74px and width 430px;
- collapsed width retained: 230px.

The same-worktree 390px render measured left 12px, right 378.4px, and width
366.4px. The selector remained expanded, readable, and fully contained.
